## P01 — Employed dev, mid-size company
`[INTERVIEW: 2026-08-20-interview-working-dev.md, 2026-09-01-interview-backend-dev-fintech.md]` `[DATA: survey 31/54 employed]`

- **Role:** Software developer at a mid-size product company, 5–10 years experience
- **Situation:** Blocked on a concrete technical problem (TypeScript generics, Helm upgrade, infra config); deadline is tomorrow or end of week; the team's senior is unavailable
- **State of mind at entry:** Frustrated, 30–90 min already lost to docs and Discord with no resolution; time is the cost
- **Goals:** Unblocked today; real answer from someone who has done this exact thing; resume work before the deadline
- **Constraints:** Paying out of pocket (50–100 EUR ceiling) `[INTERVIEW: 2026-09-01]`; does not want to explain the expense to the employer; needs an answer within the hour; will not wait through onboarding or account setup
- **Tools:** Text only; pastes code, YAML, config; no video `[N01]`
- **Vocabulary:** "answer", "unblocked", "someone who's done this", "the hour" — not "mentor", "learning journey", "course"
- **Objections:** Price is visible but is it worth it for one question? What if they don't actually know the answer? What if they don't show up?
- **Refusals:** Anything requiring more than 5 minutes before first contact `[INTERVIEW: 2026-09-01 "jak booking trwa więcej niż pięć minut to robię rollback"]`; video; subscription; rating system they don't trust

## P02 — Freelancer / solo contractor
`[INTERVIEW: 2026-09-02-interview-freelancer-react.md]`

- **Role:** Freelance frontend developer (React/Node), 3 years solo, works from client briefs
- **Situation:** Needs to estimate or execute a scope of work involving technology not previously used at scale; estimate is due in 1–2 days; getting it wrong costs own time, not client's money
- **State of mind at entry:** Anxious about the unknown scope; aware the cost of a wrong estimate is personal
- **Goals:** A confirmed answer the same day; know the one thing they are missing ("zapominasz o auth"); send the estimate confidently
- **Constraints:** Pays alone (ceiling ~200 PLN, stated without hesitation) `[INTERVIEW: 2026-09-02-freelancer]`; client does not know they are asking; needs price before any commitment `[R05]`; needs refund same day if no-show `[R06]`; needs it today
- **Tools:** Text for bugs; call for estimates if needed; pastes code; uses public work to judge trust not ratings
- **Vocabulary:** "sprawdź jedną rzecz" (check one thing); "klient mi nie płaci za odrabianie lekcji"; "każdy ma 4.9" (everyone has 4.9 stars — why ratings fail)
- **Objections:** No-show risk with prepayment; can't tell the price upfront feels arbitrary; how do I know this person has done it?
- **Refusals:** Subscription `[INTERVIEW: "jak to subskrypcja, to nie otworzę drugi raz"]`; community; "mentorship journey"; video `[N01]`
